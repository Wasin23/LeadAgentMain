📋 Custom Domain Setup Instructions

To enable email tracking with your custom domain:
1. Point your domain/subdomain to your server's IP address
2. Set up SSL certificate (HTTPS required for email clients)
3. Configure reverse proxy (nginx/Apache) to route traffic to this app
4. Test tracking URL: https://yourdomain.com/track/open/test

For easy setup, use services like:
• ngrok (temporary tunneling)
• Cloudflare (DNS + SSL)
• Vercel or Render (hosting)
